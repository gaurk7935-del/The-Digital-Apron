first recipe : crispy garlic chilli noodles

A vibrant, fiery dish that brings maximum flavor with minimal effort. This recipe combines the punch of toasted garlic, the smoky heat of crushed chili flakes, and the savory depth of aged soy sauce. Tossed with chewy, wide wheat noodles and finished with a shower of fresh scallions, it is the ultimate comfort food for busy weeknights.
